# Executing Agile Team

This Udacity project documents The A-Team's six-sprint Agile delivery of a software-commerce MVP. It includes sprint planning and demonstrations, evolving backlog priorities, burn-down and burn-up reporting, a management BVIR, and written stakeholder responses.

The final delivery completed 131 of 136 story points (96%). The remaining five-point customer-review analysis story was deliberately deferred while the team prioritized security, responsiveness, availability, and the BOGO launch request.
