# WorldVisitz Mobile Application Agile Delivery Launch

This project defines an Agile delivery launch for WorldVisitz, a travel agency developing a mobile application for airline booking and partner-service upsells. The work consists of two presentation deliverables with speaker notes: an executive case for the transformation and an onboarding plan for the delivery team.

## Executive recommendation

The leadership presentation contrasts Agile with WorldVisitz’s existing plan-driven delivery approach and explains the Agile Manifesto, value-driven delivery, and the complementary strengths of Scrum, Kanban, and Extreme Programming (XP).

It recommends a Scrum-based delivery model for predictable two-week increments, with XP practices to address the team’s inconsistent coding standards, siloed contractor knowledge, late testing, and uneven collaboration across onshore and offshore developers. The approach supports an earlier booking-capability release, regular stakeholder feedback, and incremental expansion of airline and partner integrations rather than an 18-month all-at-once launch.

## Team onboarding plan

The onboarding presentation establishes a shared cross-functional way of working:

- A visible Definition of Done, shared ownership, pairing, and continuous quality practices.
- Sprint burndown and cumulative-flow information radiators to make progress, flow, and improvement needs visible.
- A two-week Scrum cadence with Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Sprint Review, and Retrospective agendas.
- Scrum role assignments aligned to the team’s current skills.
- A 90-day Shu Ha Ri coaching plan that combines whole-team coaching with targeted one-to-one support.
